Part One

🌱 I Owe You an Apology

Before anything else — I am sorry.

The way I spoke to you last time was wrong. Calling you stupid, questioning your sense, bringing in family honour and shame — none of that was fair and none of that was love, even if it came from worry. You deserved better from me and I did not give you that. I understand if those words hurt, and I want you to know I regret them completely.

I am not writing this to continue that fight. I am writing this because I want to start over — the right way.

Part Three

🏡 What This Family Has Quietly Carried

I think about our family a lot, Antarappa. And I want to tell you something I rarely say out loud.

Mummy — waking up at 5am or 6am every single morning. Quietly making my Tiffin before I even opened my eyes. Not once complaining. Papa — sometimes walking me to the nearest bus stop before his own day began. Then I would take my bicycle to college — through whatever weather, whatever tiredness.

Our family — together always, through everything 💙

And then came the accident. I still think about the tears you all shed that day. It was after that — Mummy decided. We will get him a scooter. That decision came from love.That scooter was not a gift. It was a prayer on wheels — from a mother who could not afford to imagine the alternative.

Part Five

💌 What I Need You to Hear

You were aiming for CA, Antarappa. That showed real ambition and I was genuinely proud. Now it is BBA. I am not saying it is wrong.

And yes — I saw that message where you used ChatGPT to frame your thoughts. I am not upset about that. These tools are powerful and I use them too. But here is the thing — AI can help you articulate what you already think. It cannot think for you. Use it to sharpen your ideas, to research, to write better — not to borrow someone else's conviction.

ChatGPT Message — borrow tools, not conviction 🤖

The day you let a tool decide what you believe, you have handed away the most valuable thing you own — your own mind.

But I want you to sit quietly — just once — and ask yourself one honest question:

Is this decision fully yours? 🤔

The people we spend time with shape our thinking more than we realise. Sometimes quietly. Sometimes without us noticing. Your circle, your friendships — they are not neutral. They leave fingerprints on your goals.

And about feelings — the closeness, the attachment — I am not here to shame you. Those feelings are real, I understand that. But when the heart gets deeply attached, the mind quietly steps aside. That is what worries me. Not your character. Never your character.

Always in your corner 🤝

You are sharp. You are creative. You are the girl who kept a chocolate aside for Dadu without being asked. You are the girl who made a photo frame and wrote "Sweet Memories" on it. You are the girl who wrote "My Mother is Best" in her own hand.

I just do not want someone else's presence to quietly rewrite the future you deserve to write yourself.
